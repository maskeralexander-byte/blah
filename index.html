View-only lock

This app never clicks, types or submits anything on the site. It only reads the tables on the page you have open and the exports you download. The lock also stops the browser window from sending anything that could change data (saves, submissions, deletes). Sign-in is the one exception. If a report won't load while locked, turn the lock off, load the report, and turn it back on.

What stands out

Column mapping

The analyzer matched your report's columns automatically. Fix any that are wrong or missing. Times like 24:35 are read as minutes:seconds; $, % and commas are ignored. Rows labeled Total are skipped so they don't double-count.

Targets

Defaults are common starting points. Set them to your franchise or district goals; they're saved in this browser.
